SkyNetworks is designed to keep agreements clear and conversations safer. Public discussions help everyone understand a task; private messages protect the details that should stay between the parties.

Read our platform rules →
◉ Public by design

Public task threads are visible to signed-in members. Avoid sharing phone numbers, email addresses, payment credentials or exact home addresses there.

▣ Private when agreed

After selecting a preferred provider, open a private thread for sensitive project details. Private does not mean immune from lawful safety review.

♢ Fair platform fees

Show the service fee before confirmation. The proposed default is a 10% provider fee, configurable by the platform owner.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Terms & conditions

This is a starter policy outline for the prototype, not legal advice. Have a Kenyan/African technology lawyer review it before launch and adapt it to each country you serve.

1. Platform role

SkyNetworks introduces customers and independent service providers. Unless a separate written service is offered, the platform is not the employer, contractor or guarantor of either party.

2. Fees & payments

The platform must display any service fee, who pays it, taxes and refund rules before a user accepts an offer. Do not collect money through this demo.

3. Conduct & contact details

No harassment, fraud, spam, threats or attempts to bypass agreed platform fees. Public posts must not contain phone numbers, email addresses, payment details or personal addresses.

4. Privacy & moderation

Collect only necessary data, explain retention and deletion, restrict staff access, secure accounts and provide a way to report content. Private messages may be reviewed only under a disclosed, lawful safety process.

5. Disputes & cancellations

Users should document scope, price, deadlines and milestones in the agreement. Publish a fair complaint, cancellation, refund and escalation process before enabling transactions.

6. Reviews & account action

Reviews must reflect genuine experiences. SkyNetworks may investigate reports and restrict accounts for policy violations, with an appeal route where appropriate.

Privacy notice, cookie notice, prohibited-items policy, provider standards and country-specific consumer protection terms should be separate linked documents in production.
